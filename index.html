01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I’m fascinated by the questions that sit at the boundary of what we know — from the information paradox of black holes to the possibilities of quantum technologies.

I hold a BS in Physics and am pursuing an MS in Physics. My academic interests include black holes, cosmology, relativity, quantum information, and quantum machine learning.

Alongside research, I work in science education at The Black Hole in Islamabad, helping young learners engage with science through curiosity, experimentation, and observation.

01 / ABOUT ME

Curiosity has always
been my starting point.

I hold a BS degree in Physics and am currently pursuing an MS in Applied Physics at Air University, Islamabad.

During my undergraduate degree, I was offered the opportunity to design an Astronomy course for PolyMath-Kids. That opportunity grew into a teaching role in which I have taught Astronomy, Python, C++, Data Science, and Machine Learning for children. I continue to teach at the platform.

My BS Final Year Project focused on understanding the Black Hole Information Paradox. Driven by that interest, I independently studied Special and General Relativity through MIT OpenCourseWare.

In my final undergraduate year, I served as President of the Air Astronomical Society. I received a medal from the Vice Chancellor of Air University in recognition of my efforts to promote space sciences among students and the wider public.
